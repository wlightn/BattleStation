# Tournament Workflow

## Purpose

This document defines the high-level event flow for BattleStation System 1.0.

## Workflow

1. Registration
   - Competitors enter driver and robot information.

2. Inspection
   - Robots are checked for weight, safety, and class compliance.

3. Bracket Generation
   - BattleStation generates the tournament bracket from approved entries.

4. Call First Drivers to Arena
   - The first scheduled drivers are called to the arena.

5. Introductions
   - BattleStation presents the current match and introduces the drivers/robots.

6. Driver Ready
   - Drivers press Ready at their driver stations.

7. Match
   - The match timer runs.
   - Safety systems are monitored.
   - Pause, tap out, and unstick events are tracked.

8. Results
   - The winner is recorded.
   - The bracket advances automatically.

9. Arena Reset / Next Match Delay
   - A short delay allows the arena to be reset.
   - BattleStation displays a timer between matches.
   - The next drivers are called to the arena.

10. Repeat Match Cycle
   - Steps 5 through 9 repeat until the tournament is complete.

11. Awards
   - Final placements and awards are presented.

12. Reports
   - BattleStation compiles final standings, completed brackets, match history, and event reports.

## Notes

- The between-match timer should help keep the event moving.
- The public display should show the current match, next match, and reset timer when appropriate.
- Reports are generated at the end of the tournament.
