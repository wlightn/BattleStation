# Purpose

This document defines how BattleStation Engineering applies the official Battle of Bots branding and visual identity throughout the BattleStation system.

The BattleStation Style Guide does not establish branding.

Brand identity is established and governed by the Battle of Bots Marketing Department.

This document defines the engineering application of those approved standards.

---

# Scope

This guide applies to all BattleStation engineering deliverables, including:

- Software user interfaces
- Hardware enclosures
- Hardware labels
- Printed materials generated by BattleStation
- Public Display
- Mission Control
- Documentation
- Event reports
- Future BattleStation hardware modules

---

# Governing Marketing Standards

BattleStation Engineering shall comply with the following Battle of Bots constitutional marketing documents.

- BOB-REL-001
- BOB-STD-001
- BOB-STD-002
- BOB-STD-003

Where conflicts exist, the Marketing standards shall take precedence regarding brand identity.

Engineering standards govern only technical implementation.

---

# Engineering Responsibility

Engineering is responsible for:

- Correct application of approved branding.
- Consistent implementation across BattleStation.
- Technical integration of brand assets.
- Maintaining image quality.
- Preserving readability.
- Supporting accessibility where practical.

Engineering shall not redefine:

- Logos
- Colors
- Typography
- Brand identity
- Marketing artwork
- Official messaging

Those responsibilities remain under Marketing stewardship.

# Engineering Application Standards

BattleStation shall apply approved Battle of Bots branding consistently across all engineering deliverables.

Engineering implementation shall preserve the intent of the published Marketing standards while satisfying technical and operational requirements.

---

# Software User Interfaces

BattleStation user interfaces shall:

- Use approved Battle of Bots logos where appropriate.
- Apply approved colors consistently.
- Preserve readability under tournament operating conditions.
- Maintain consistent visual presentation across all interfaces.
- Avoid distortion or modification of approved brand assets.

Mission Control, Registration Station, Inspection Station, and Public Display shall present a unified visual identity.

---

# Hardware Identification

BattleStation hardware shall be clearly identified using standardized engineering labels.

Hardware identification may include:

- BattleStation module name
- Module identifier
- Revision information
- Asset identification
- Required safety markings

Application of Battle of Bots branding shall comply with the published Marketing standards.

Engineering identification markings shall remain distinct from marketing branding.

---

# Printed Material

BattleStation-generated printed material shall present a consistent professional appearance.

Examples include:

- Registration forms
- Inspection forms
- Match sheets
- Event reports
- Final standings
- Awards documentation

Printed material shall apply approved branding without reducing clarity or readability.

---

# Public Display

The Public Display represents the public face of BattleStation during tournament operation.

It shall:

- Apply approved Battle of Bots branding.
- Present tournament information clearly.
- Prioritize readability over decorative elements.
- Maintain a consistent appearance throughout the event.

Operational information shall always take precedence over decorative graphics.

---

# Documentation

Engineering documentation shall remain primarily technical.

Branding within engineering documentation shall:

- Clearly identify the project.
- Maintain a professional appearance.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative elements.
- Preserve readability for engineering review.

Marketing materials remain the responsibility of the Marketing Department.

---

# Asset Usage

Engineering shall use only approved released assets.

Engineering shall not modify:

- Official logos
- Brand colors
- Typography
- Marketing artwork
- Official graphics

If technical constraints require a variation of an approved asset, Engineering shall coordinate with Marketing before implementation.

---

# Accessibility

Engineering implementations should remain usable under typical tournament operating conditions.

Where practical:

- Text shall remain readable.
- Color shall not be the sole indicator of important information.
- Critical operational information shall remain clearly distinguishable from decorative elements.

# Style Guide Governance

The BattleStation Style Guide governs the engineering application of approved Battle of Bots branding.

This document shall not duplicate or replace the official Marketing standards.

Engineering shall reference the published Marketing standards as the authoritative source for:

- Brand identity
- Logos
- Typography
- Color palette
- Graphic assets
- Brand messaging

Changes to Battle of Bots branding shall be made only through the appropriate Marketing governance process.

Engineering implementation shall be updated to reflect approved Marketing releases.

---

# Configuration Management

All branding assets used by BattleStation shall originate from approved released Marketing assets.

Engineering shall reference controlled assets rather than maintaining independent copies whenever practical.

Released assets shall be:

- Traceable
- Version controlled
- Configuration managed
- Referenced by release identifier where appropriate

Engineering-generated derivatives, such as software icons, panel layouts, hardware labels, and documentation graphics, shall preserve the integrity of the approved source assets.

---

# Design Principles

BattleStation shall present a professional and consistent appearance.

Engineering implementation shall:

- Respect approved Marketing standards.
- Preserve technical clarity.
- Prioritize operational usability.
- Maintain visual consistency.
- Use only approved released assets.
- Clearly distinguish engineering information from marketing content.

Visual presentation shall support tournament operation rather than distract from it.
