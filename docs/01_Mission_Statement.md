# BattleStation Mission Statement

## Mission

BattleStation is a complete, modular robot combat event management system designed to automate tournament operations, reduce coordinator workload, improve safety, and provide a professional event experience.

BattleStation shall look, feel, and operate like a commercial event management system rather than a collection of unrelated hobby electronics.

The system shall be reliable enough to operate the February 2027 Battle of Bots tournament while remaining flexible enough to support future classes, statistics, modules, event formats, and organizational growth.

BattleStation is a project within the wLIGHTn organization and applies published organizational standards while remaining responsible for the engineering and operation of the BattleStation system.
