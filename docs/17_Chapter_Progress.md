# Purpose

This document records the engineering progress of BattleStation throughout its development lifecycle.

Chapter Progress provides a high-level view of completed milestones, current work, and major engineering accomplishments.

Unlike Git commit history, which records individual implementation changes, this document records significant engineering progress.

Each completed Chapter establishes a new constitutional engineering baseline for future development.

---

# Progress Philosophy

BattleStation development proceeds through sequential engineering Chapters.

Each Chapter:

- Defines specific engineering objectives.
- Produces measurable engineering deliverables.
- Concludes with an engineering review.
- Establishes a stable baseline for future work.

Engineering progress shall emphasize quality, completeness, and traceability rather than implementation speed.

# Engineering History

BattleStation is developed using a discovery-driven engineering process.

Major architectural concepts are expected to emerge through engineering discussion, implementation experience, and operational observation.

Significant discoveries shall be documented, reviewed, and incorporated into the constitutional architecture where appropriate.

The objective is not to invent an architecture, but to discover the architecture that best satisfies the enduring responsibilities of the BattleStation System.

As constitutional documents mature, they establish progressively more stable engineering baselines while remaining open to future discoveries supported by objective engineering evidence.

---

# Progress Governance

Chapter Progress records engineering maturity.

It is not intended to replace:

- Git commit history
- Release notes
- Engineering Decision Records
- Project Journal

Those documents answer different engineering questions.

Chapter Progress provides a high-level view of where BattleStation currently stands within its planned engineering lifecycle.

As Chapters are completed, this document becomes the historical record of BattleStation's engineering evolution.

---

# Design Principles

Engineering progress shall:

- Follow the approved Development Roadmap.
- Produce measurable engineering deliverables.
- Preserve constitutional engineering baselines.
- Record significant engineering discoveries.
- Support objective engineering review.
- Maintain traceability between Chapters.
- Encourage continuous improvement through discovery.
